# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: May 3, 2026**

At ControlApp, the privacy and security of your business and personal data are our priority. This policy details how we collect, protect, and use your information when using our platform and mobile applications.

## 1. Information We Collect

For the operation of ControlApp, we collect the following categories of information:

*   **Account Data**: Name, email address, and profile photo.
*   **Financial Data**: Account names, balances, expense categories, transaction amounts, and credit cutoff dates.
*   **Operational Data**: Production processes, stages, batches (lots), input recipes, and inventory records.
*   **AI Interactions**: Messages and queries sent through the AI assistant.
*   **Third-Party Credentials**: AI provider API keys (stored in encrypted form).

## 2. Data Safety

We implement industrial standards to protect your information:

*   **Encryption in Transit**: All data is transmitted via SSL/TLS (HTTPS) protocols.
*   **Encryption at Rest**: Sensitive information, such as third-party API keys, is stored encrypted in our database.
*   **Data Isolation**: Your data is private. Other users can only see information from projects to which you have explicitly invited them.
*   **Data Deletion**: When you delete your account, all your personal projects and associated records are deleted in cascade.

## 3. Information Use and AI Integration

We use your information solely to provide the service. If you enable AI features:
*   Your queries (prompts) are sent to providers such as **OpenAI, Google (Gemini), or Anthropic** for processing.
*   We do not sell or rent your information to third parties for advertising purposes.

## 4. User Rights

In accordance with data protection laws (GDPR), you have the right to:
1.  **Access** your personal data at any time.
2.  **Rectify** inaccurate information.
3.  **Delete** your data and your account definitively from the profile panel.

## 5. Google Play Store Compliance

This application meets the data transparency requirements of Google Play. We do not request location permissions, access to contacts, or sensitive device information other than what is strictly necessary for the described business management functionality.
